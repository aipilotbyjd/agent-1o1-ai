# Create a Team

Create a team only when several people need to own agents and connectors together. For most rollouts, keep the agent personal and [share it](/help/sharing/let-someone-edit-an-agent).

## Before you start

You need an organization **Admin** or **Manager** role. See [Organization and Teams](/core-concepts/teams).

## From the sidebar

1. On the [Home page](https://www.gumloop.com/hub), find **Teams** in the left sidebar.
2. Click the **+** icon.
3. Name the team, optionally pick a logo and brand color, then click **Create**.

## From organization settings

1. Go to [Organization > Teams](https://www.gumloop.com/settings/organization/teams).
2. Click **Create**.

## After you create it

* [Add people](/help/sharing/add-someone-to-org-or-team) to the team.
* [Add team connectors](/help/connectors/add-team-connectors) if the team should share one account.
* Move or create agents in that team only when they should be team-owned.

<Tip>
  You do not need a team just to let someone use an agent. Share the agent with **Use Only** or **Editor** instead.
</Tip>
